Method (`itb_trace.py`, `itb_zone.py`): each zone is a list of waypoints (OSM road junctions found by intersecting the named ways, OSM places, ISTAT border points) joined by legs: "road" legs are shortest paths on the OSM road graph with the named roads weighted 1 and all other roads 6 (so gaps in the named way are bridged by real roads), "stream" legs follow OSM waterways, "border" legs follow ISTAT comune or province boundaries, "line" legs are straight (used for "piede della falda", mule tracks, contour lines and unmapped farm roads). The loop is noded and polygonised, then opened by 10 m (5 m in Valtellina) to remove slivers, simplified at 20 m (10 m for Valtellina and FCO) and rounded to 5 decimals. Overlaps where texts share an edge were removed afterwards (`itb_t03_overlaps.py`; Predappio and Oriolo minus Castrocaro, Marzeno minus Modigliana, Longiano minus Mercato Saraceno, Valgella minus Inferno). Re-running `itb_t03_assemble.py` must be followed by `itb_t03_overlaps.py`.

Quality: these are honest approximations. Positions of the lines are right to the road or stream, but where the text names private tracks, houses or spot heights missing from OSM the edge is straight, so a few hundred metres of error are possible, especially in Brisighella, Predappio, Mercato Saraceno, Meldola, Valtellina and Rosazzo. The "contrade" of Valtellina follow the foot of the slope only roughly. Verdicchio Classico and the two commune FCO units inherit the commune-union outer edge of their parents. Remaining overlaps under 1%: Cesena / Mercato Saraceno 0.2 km².

Ids: all existing ids were kept (all 26 were points in `_extra_points.geojson` and are now polygons). Parents set to the legal parent (it-romagna, it-valtellina-superiore, it-friuli-colli-orientali, it-verdicchio-dei-castelli-di-jesi) instead of the containment parents the points had (it-colli-romagna-centrale, it-friuli-grave, ...). The old Cialla point sat at 13.16 E, 25 km off.

Not drawn: **San Vicinio** (Romagna). It is not in the consolidated text (16 sottozone: the 12 of 2011 with Mercato Saraceno in place of San Vicinio, plus Imola, Coriano, San Clemente, Verucchio). Bottles labelled "Romagna San Vicinio" appear up to the 2019 vintage (e.g. https://dmvini.ch/en/monte-sasso-romagna-san-vicinio-doc_16668719) and "Mercato Saraceno" after (https://vertdevin.com/en/appellation/romagna-mercato-saraceno), so San Vicinio looks like the former name of Mercato Saraceno; added as alt there. **Pignolo di Rosazzo** has no separate feature (same area as Ribolla Gialla di Rosazzo; see above). Ramandolo is its own DOCG (it-ramandolo, already a polygon in it.geojson).

## T04: Roero and Dogliani MGAs (225 polygons)

**Source used: official parcel lists, not consortium maps.** The Piemonte geoportal (geoportale.igr.piemonte.it, geoportale.piemonte.it) still did not answer and the Consorzio Barolo Barbaresco site timed out (consorziodelroero.it returned 403), so no consortium map was traced and no georeferencing was needed (no `georef.md`). Instead, the località MGAs are delimited in the disciplinari by cadastral parcels (Roero Allegato 2B, Dogliani Allegato 1B), and the Agenzia delle Entrate INSPIRE WFS serves those parcels.

Method:
1. `itb_mga_parse.py`: the annex tables of `raw/it_disciplinari/Roero.pdf` and `Dogliani.pdf` (MASAF texts mirrored by AIS) read with pdfplumber, row by row between the table rules (the plain table extractor drops rows at page tops; the band parser recovers them). Roero: 273 rows (MGA, comune, foglio, particelle), 25,989 parcel numbers; Dogliani: 165 rows, 23,175 parcel numbers. Output `work/italy-bounds/mga_{roero,dogliani}.json`.
2. `itb_t04_fetch.py` / `itb_cadastre.py`: fogli (CadastralZoning) of the comuni, comune code by overlap with ISTAT (e.g. Canale B573, Vezza d'Alba L817, Dogliani D314), then the parcels (CadastralParcel) of each listed foglio in 0.009 degree tiles; parcels matched by number. Cache: `raw/italy-bounds/ae_cadastre/` (gzip GML, 31 MB+).
3. `itb_t04_build.py`: union of the matched parcels per MGA, closed by 8 m to bridge the road and ditch gaps between parcels (without spilling onto a neighbouring MGA), holes under 0.2 ha removed, simplified at 8 m, 5 decimals. bq `official` when at least 90% of the listed parcels were found in today's cadastre, else `approx` (renumbered parcels since the annex was written). src `aecad`.
4. Comune MGAs (Allegato part A: 18 Roero comuni, 21 Dogliani comuni), existing subcat "MGA (commune)": ISTAT 2026 comune clipped to the atlas DOCG polygon, bq `commune`, src `istatcom`.

Results:
- Roero: 131 of 133 località MGAs drawn (97 official, 34 approx) + 18 comune MGAs. 23,781 of 25,566 listed parcels matched (93.0%). Lowest match: Prachiosso 73%, Betlemme 79%, San Carlo, Volta 81%.
- Dogliani: all 55 località MGAs drawn (36 official, 19 approx) + 21 comune MGAs. 20,975 of 23,175 parcels (90.5%). Lowest: San Sebastiano 74%, Pianbosco 76%.
- Sizes: Roero 0.02 to 1.45 km² (median 0.22), Dogliani 0.04 to 7.0 km² (median 0.5).
- Not drawn (Roero): **Corso** (in the Allegato 2 list but no row in Allegato 2B), **Tavolato Colla** (the list has "Tavolato Colla"; the table has a "Colla" in Vezza d'Alba, fogli 7, 10, 11, and a "Tavoleto" in Sommariva Perno, foglio 21, two places in non-adjacent comuni; left for a human decision). Table rows with no id: Colla, Tavoleto (above) and "Roncaglia" in Castellinaldo d'Alba (fogli 2, 3; the list only has Roncaglie in Canale and Monticello).
- Name fixes table to list: Bricco di Piobesi to Bricco di Piovesi, Pecetto to Precetto, San Siverio to San Silverio, Serra Zoanni to Serra Zanni, "Madonna delle" and "Santa" (names broken over a page) to Madonna delle Grazie and Santa Margherita. The San Michele rows in Santo Stefano Roero join it-roero-san-michele-monteu-roero (their parcels touch it; Canale's is 3 km away).
- Overlaps over 2% between MGAs of one parent, all from parcels listed under two MGAs in the annex (or the Zoanni/Zanni row): Dogliani Corradini/Zovetto 2.3%, Corradini/Masanti 2.1%; Roero Bric Valdiana/Valteppe 33%, Bric del Medic/Valteppe 10%, Bric del Medic/Bric Valdiana 8% (Montà foglio 10, 13 to 28 shared parcels), San Carlo/Serra Zanni 44% (Castellinaldo foglio 10, row spelled "Serra Zoanni"). Kept as the law lists them.
- Parents set to it-roero / it-dogliani (many points had containment parents such as it-cisterna-d-asti, it-barbaresco, it-barletta). All 133 + 55 ids are the existing ones; the MGA points were removed from `_extra_points.geojson`.

## Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi Classico
*art. 3 (last paragraph; the zone itself is the preceding paragraph of art. 3)*

> L'uso della menzione "Classico" è riservata al vino ottenuto dalle uve raccolte nella zona originaria più antica. Tale zona è costituita da quella delimitata dal presente articolo con l'esclusione dei territori posti alla sinistra del Fiume Misa e dei territori appartenenti ai comuni di Ostra e di Senigallia in provincia di Ancona.
