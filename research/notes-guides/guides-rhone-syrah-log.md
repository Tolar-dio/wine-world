## Structure

- **southern-rhone** (region). Intro: valley/mistral, Grenache and partners, Châteauneuf history, soils, the pyramid and value crus, Gigondas/Vacqueyras in 3D, Tavel/Lirac/VDN. Deeper: lieux-dits, traditional vs modern producers, white Châteauneuf, vintages/heat/ageing.
  Flight: Côtes du Rhône ($12–28) → named Villages ($25–30) → Châteauneuf ($50–70) → Gigondas ($45–60), with a Vacqueyras next to the Gigondas to show the effect of altitude. Splurges are in the deeper part.
- **syrah** (grape, 9 intro chapters). Flight A: Crozes-Hermitage (ch3, $25–35) vs Barossa Shiraz (ch5, $20–33). Flight B, cool climate on three continents: Canberra (ch6), coastal California (ch8), Elqui/Limarí (ch9). Hawke's Bay and the Swartland are extra comparisons.
- 3D shots: Gigondas/Dentelles, Vacqueyras, Côte-Rôtie (bearing 315), Hermitage (bearing 0), Cornas (bearing 285), Alcohuaz/Elqui (bearing 180). Satellite shots: La Crau, Rayas, Beaucastel, Freedom 1843, Gimblett Gravels, the Rocks.

## Errors and inconsistencies found in the atlas (not edited)

1. **fr_fine.json `fr-chateauneuf-du-pape-la-crau`**: the summary says the telegraph tower was "built in the 1790s". The producer says Claude Chappe built it in **1821**, and `site-fr-domaine-du-vieux-telegraphe` in the atlas also says 1821. The nerd_note says the Bruniers have farmed La Crau "since 1898". The producer gives 1891 for the plots Henri gave Hippolyte, while retailers repeat 1898. I wrote "since the 1890s".
2. **fr_fine.json `fr-chateauneuf-du-pape-le-rayas`** ("since 1997 ... Emmanuel Reynaud has run it") and **fr.json site `site-fr-chateau-rayas`** ("Emmanuel Reynaud's mythical estate") are out of date. Emmanuel Reynaud died on 25 Nov 2025 (Wine Spectator, 26 Nov 2025), and his sons succeed him.
3. **fr.json `fr-muscat-de-beaumes-de-venise`**: the profile says "at least 110 g/l of sugar". The INAO cahier des charges says **≥100 g/l**.
4. **fr.json `fr-tavel`** nerd_note: "several days of maceration" looks overstated. Tavel macerations are usually counted in hours, up to a day or two. I could not verify this before the search budget ran out, so the guide just says "longer on the skins".
5. **grapes.json `syrah`**: `offspring` lists Mondeuse Noire. The `mondeuse-blanche` entry calls Mondeuse Noire a *half-sibling* of Syrah (Mondeuse Blanche × Tressot). The two entries contradict each other, and I believe the half-sibling version is the right one. I did not use this in the guide.
6. **_regions.geojson `region-fr-southern-rhone`**: its bbox [3.837, 43.62, 5.734, 45.52] is the same as the whole Rhône Valley bbox and reaches to Vienne. Flying to that id would frame the north too, so I used coordinates instead.
7. **Tablas Creek founding date**: the atlas site says 1989 and Wikipedia says 1990. Both guides avoid a single year ("at the end of the 1980s" / "later").
8. **fr_fine.json `fr-chateauneuf-du-pape-la-roquette`**: the summary puts it "on the plateau east of the village, on galets over clay", but the polygon's label point (4.8404, 44.0745) is northeast, next to Rayas and Pignan in the sandy zone. The text and the shape may not match. I did not use it.
9. **fr.json `fr-gigondas`**: "since September 2022, white Gigondas ... has also been allowed". INAO voted in September 2022, but the rule applies **from the 2023 vintage**. Worth rewording.
10. Minor point: the Muscat BdV syndicate page phrases the mutage figure ambiguously ("when it contains 5 to 10% alcohol"). The Rasteau VDN page says added spirit must be 5–10% of the final volume. I avoided the number.
