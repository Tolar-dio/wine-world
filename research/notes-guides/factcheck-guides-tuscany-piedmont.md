## Writer's open items

- **2026 Chianti rules: confirmed.**
  - Wein.plus: the minimum Sangiovese drops from 70% to 60%. The Terre di Vinci subzone covers Vinci, Cerreto Guidi, Fucecchio and Capraia e Limite.
  - Vinetur (16 June 2026): the Ministry approved the rules and published them in the Gazzetta. They add a Chianti rosé, the zone covers more than 13,600 ha, and it sells about 75 million bottles a year.
  - Eight subzones (seven plus Terre di Vinci) matches the atlas.
- **Gran Selezione: confirmed** (Italian Wine Central, zonation article).
  - At least 90% Sangiovese, with only native partners, from the 2027 vintage (released in 2030).
  - Lamole, Montefioralle and Vagliagli take effect from January 2027, with a one-off choice of name.
  - UGAs are retroactive for GS wines still ageing.
