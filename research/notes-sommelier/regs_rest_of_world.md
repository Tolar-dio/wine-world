# Production and labelling rules outside France, Italy, Spain and Portugal (current to October 2026)

## Germany: Praedikat must weights, QbA/Landwein, the origin pyramid and 2026 "Region" rule, GG/EG in law, Sekt, Eiswein, sweetness, Anbaugebiete and single-site PDOs

### Takeaway
German wine law sets national floors (Zone A Qualitaetswein at least 7.0% potential and Praedikatswein 9.5%; Zone B 8.0% and 10.0%), but the real must weights, yields and site rules now sit in the 13 Anbaugebiet PDO specifications. The 2021 origin pyramid is binding from the 2026 vintage, and a Bereich or Grosslage must then carry the prefix "Region". The 13th amending ordinance of 2026 makes Grosses Gewaechs and Erstes Gewaechs federal quality marks, awarded by a joint DWV/VDP classification committee, with old rules running on until the 2030 vintage.

### Cited Findings
- Federal categories, natural-alcohol floors, Praedikat definitions, bottling terms (Erzeugerabfuellung, Gutsabfuellung, Schlossabfuellung) and Riesling-Hochgewaechs ([WeinG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/weing_1994/); [WeinV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/weinv_1995/)).
- Bundesrat approval on 12 June 2026 of the 13th ordinance amending the WeinV. It rewrites s. 30 so that GG and Erstes Gewaechs are awarded by the "Komitee Klassifikation Erster und Grosser Lagen in Deutschland e.V.", with transitional rules to the 2030 vintage and Classic and Cremant transitions to 2028 ([Bundesrat Drucksache 272/26](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brd/2026/0272-26B.pdf)).
- New origin pyramid: Anbaugebiet, then Region, Ortswein and Lagenwein, with release dates of 15 December for Ortswein and 1 March for Lagenwein ([Wines of Germany: new origin pyramid](https://www.winesofgermany.com/our-wine/quality-standards/quality-standard/462/new-origin-pyramid-for-qualit%C3%A4t-and-pr%C3%A4dikat-wines)).
- Mosel minimums: QbA Riesling and Elbling 55 Oe; Kabinett 70 to 73 Oe; Spaetlese 80 to 85; Auslese 88 to 93; BA 110; TBA 150; Eiswein 110. Maximum yield 125 hl/ha. Riesling or Spaetburgunder 85% for site names in the Saar, Ruwertal, Bernkastel and Burg Cochem Bereiche ([BLE: Mosel product specification](https://www.ble.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Ernaehrung-Lebensmittel/EU-Qualitaetskennzeichen/Wein/Antraege/Bestimmte_Anbaugebiete/01_Produktspezifiaktion_Anbaugebiete/Produktspezifikation_Mosel.pdf?__blob=publicationFile)).
- Rheingau: QbA whites 57 Oe; Auslese Riesling 95 Oe; 100 hl/ha. A proposed amendment would limit Einzellage wines to listed varieties at 85% ([BLE: Rheingau specification](https://www.ble.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Ernaehrung-Lebensmittel/EU-Qualitaetskennzeichen/Wein/Antraege/Bestimmte_Anbaugebiete/01_Produktspezifiaktion_Anbaugebiete/Produktspezifikation_Rheingau.pdf?__blob=publicationFile)).
- Baden (Zone B): variety-specific minimums (e.g. Spaetburgunder QbA 69, Kabinett 85, Spaetlese 95 Oe); BA 128, TBA 154; 90 hl/ha ([BLE: Baden specification](https://www.ble.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Ernaehrung-Lebensmittel/EU-Qualitaetskennzeichen/Wein/Antraege/Bestimmte_Anbaugebiete/01_Produktspezifiaktion_Anbaugebiete/Produktspezifikation_Baden.pdf?__blob=publicationFile)).
- Single-site PDO Uhlen Roth Lay: Riesling only, 70 hl/ha, at least 88 Oe for QbA and 105 for Praedikat; Kabinett and Spaetlese banned; long list of banned practices ([BLE: PS Uhlen Roth Lay](https://www.ble.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Ernaehrung-Lebensmittel/EU-Qualitaetskennzeichen/Wein/Antraege/Kleinere_gU/01_Produktspezifikationen_Kleinere_gU/PS_Uhlen_Roth_Lay.pdf?__blob=publicationFile)).
- VDP classification (Gutswein, Ortswein, Erste Lage, Grosse Lage) is private ([VDP classification](https://www.vdp.de/en/the-wines/classification)).
- EU sweetness terms used on German labels ([Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/33](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2019/33/oj)).

### Inferences
- From 2026 a "Region" prefix on Bereich and Grosslage names, together with the ban on commune plus Grosslage, removes the old trick of Grosslage names that look like villages (e.g. Piesporter Michelsberg style names). This follows from WeinV s. 39 as amended, and I have not seen it in market practice yet.
- Because the GG and EG criteria have not yet been published under the new committee, cards show the pre-September-2026 rules as current.

### Gaps
- Final criteria of the new GG/EG classification committee (not yet adopted as of October 2026).
- The status of the pending Mosel (Likoerwein) and Rheingau/Rheinhessen specification amendments; they are recorded as "proposed" or "in progress".

## Austria: DACs, KMW, Praedikat levels, Vinea Wachau, Ruster Ausbruch, Wiener Gemischter Satz

### Takeaway
Austria measures must weight in KMW (Qualitaetswein 15, Kabinett 17, Spaetlese 19, Auslese 21, BA and Eiswein 25, TBA 30) and caps yields at 10,000 kg or 7,500 L per ha. The DAC decrees, now covering every main area, set the varieties, tiers (Gebiets, Orts, Riedenwein), sugar caps and release dates. In October 2026 the Constitutional Court annulled the 2023 rule that let the National Wine Committee classify Erste and Grosse Lagen.

### Cited Findings
- KMW thresholds, Kabinett rules (dry, max 12.9%), Praedikat release dates, Strohwein drying, "Ausbruch" reserved for Rust, Bergwein, Heuriger and Schilcher definitions ([Weingesetz 2009, RIS](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20006524)).
- Yield cap of 10,000 kg / 7,500 L per ha, Reserve rules and the Erste/Grosse Lage provision WBV s. 1a ([Weinbezeichnungsverordnung, RIS](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20007217)).
- The VfGH annulled WBV s. 1a in October 2026; the ministry said no Ried had been classified under it ([news.at](https://www.news.at/wirtschaft/vfgh-stoppt-rieden-einstufung-durch-nationales-weinkomitees)).
- Sekt g.U. tiers: Sekt Austria (9 months on lees), Reserve (18 months, traditional method), Grosse Reserve (36 months, single commune) ([Sekt-Verordnung, RIS](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20011804)).
- Wachau DAC (from the 2020 vintage): Riedenwein only Gruener Veltliner or Riesling, everything hand-harvested, no enrichment for Riedenwein ([Wachau DAC decree](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20011163)). Vinea Wachau styles: Steinfeder up to 11.5%, Federspiel up to 12.5%, Smaragd at least 12.5% ([Vinea Wachau](https://vinea-wachau.at/en/translate-to-englisch-vinea-wachau/vinea-wine-styles)).
- Ruster Ausbruch DAC: at least 30 KMW, botrytised berries from Rust, not submitted before 1 April ([Ruster Ausbruch DAC decree](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20011292)).
- Wiener Gemischter Satz DAC: at least three white varieties from one registered vineyard, largest max 50%, third at least 10% ([WGS DAC decree](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20008544)).
- Weinviertel DAC (first DAC, 2002): Gruener Veltliner, max 6 g/l, DAC capsule ([Weinviertel DAC decree](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20006693)). Vulkanland Steiermark DAC (renamed in 2018) ([Vulkanland decree](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20010439)).

### Inferences
- The ministry has said the pending Weingesetz reform will move site classification into producer-group specifications. Until then the OeTW "Erste Lage" stays a private mark, and no legal Erste or Grosse Lage exists.

### Gaps
- austrianwine.com returned 403, so the official industry summaries were not used; all cards rest on the RIS legal texts.
- The VfGH ruling is reported via news.at; the judgment text itself was not fetched.

## Hungary: Tokaj in depth, Eger and Egri Bikaver, Villany

### Takeaway
Tokaj's specification (version 10, harvests from 1 August 2025) sets a 120 g/l floor for Aszu, still allows "6 puttonyos" only at 150 g/l and above, and requires 18 months in cask and local bottling in the Tokaji bottle. Egri Bikaver needs at least four varieties, with Kekfrankos 30 to 65%. Villany reserves Super Premium for 100% Cabernet Franc labelled "Villanyi Franc".

### Cited Findings
- Tokaj varieties (Furmint, Harslevelu, Kabar, Koverszolo, Sargamuskotaly, Zeta), style minimums (e.g. Eszencia at least 450 g/l; Aszu 19% total, at least 9% actual, 120 g/l), yields, ageing (Aszu 18 months in wood; Szaraz Szamorodni 24 months), release dates and bottling rules ([Tokaj PDO specification v10](https://boraszat.kormany.hu/download/b/85/53000/TOKAJ%20term%C3%A9kle%C3%ADr%C3%A1s%20%2810%20v%C3%A1ltozat%29%20kn.pdf)).
- Eger tiers Classicus, Superior and Grand Superior with 100, 60 and 35 hl/ha ([Eger PDO specification v6](https://boraszat.kormany.hu/download/d/c7/63000/Eger_OEM_term%C3%A9kle%C3%ADr%C3%A1s_v6.pdf)). Egri Bikaver blend rules and ageing by tier ([Egri Bikaver specification](https://gi.kormany.hu/download/2/30/73000/EGRI%20BIKAV%C3%89R%20term%C3%A9kle%C3%ADr%C3%A1s_honlapra.pdf)).
- Villany Classicus, Premium and Super Premium (5 t/ha, at least 2 years with 1 in wood, Cabernet Franc only) ([Villany PDO specification v5](https://boraszat.kormany.hu/download/6/6c/62000/VILL%C3%81NY_term%C3%A9kle%C3%ADr%C3%A1s%205-v%C3%A1ltozat%20kn.pdf)).

### Inferences
- With a 120 g/l floor, the old 3 and 4 puttonyos levels can no longer be met, so in practice puttonyos survives only as "6 puttonyos" on the sweetest Aszu.

### Gaps
- The other Hungarian districts (e.g. Somlo, Badacsony, Szekszard) were not carded.

## Greece and Cyprus (Commandaria)

### Takeaway
Greek PDOs are narrow, variety-defined appellations (e.g. Naoussa 100% Xinomavro; Nemea 100% Agiorgitiko; Santorini at least 85% Assyrtiko for the dry white), with Reserve and Grande Reserve ageing set in each specification. Commandaria's 2017 amendment raised the yield to 6,000 kg/ha and the minimum vine age to 5 years, and allowed fortification with neutral alcohol and irrigation with approval.

### Cited Findings
- Santorini: dry white at least 85% Assyrtiko, at least 12%; 50 hl/ha; Vinsanto from sun-dried grapes with at least 24 months in oak and age statements in four-year steps; Nykteri ([Santorini PDO single document](https://www.minagric.gr/images/stories/docs/agrotis/POP-PGE/TEXNIKOI%20FAKELOI%20OINON%20POP-PGE%20ENGLISH/PDO%2031/eniaio_eggrafo_POPSantorini_201221.pdf)).
- PDO variety rules for 32 appellations, e.g. Naoussa ([Wines of Greece: PDO Naoussa](https://winesofgreece.org/appellation/pdo-naoussa/)) and Mantinia, at least 85% Moschofilero ([Wines of Greece: PDO Mantinia](https://winesofgreece.org/appellation/pdo-mantinia/)). Yields come from the EU PDO dataset ([Candiago et al. 2022, figshare](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.5877659)).
- Grande Reserve (Eidika Epilegmenos) is a protected Greek traditional term ([GOV.UK protected names](https://www.gov.uk/protected-food-drink-names/eidika-epilegmeno-grande-reserve)).
- Commandaria 2017 amendment (Decree KDP 314/2016): vine age 5 years (was 4), 6,000 kg/ha (was 4,500), harvest sugar 204 g/l Xynisteri and 230 g/l Mavro, at least 374 g/l after drying, base wine 9.5%, VA up to 30 meq/l, irrigation with approval, neutral alcohol allowed for fortification, at least 2 years in oak. The village list includes Agios Georgios Silikou ([eAmbrosia: Commandaria amendment PDO-CY-A1622-AM01](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/47638)). Background on the 1990 law and the 14 villages ([Wikipedia: Commandaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commandaria)).

### Inferences
- The amended Commandaria single document still prints the older sugar figures (212/258 g/l, 390 to 450 g/l, 10%) next to the amendment's new ones. The card gives both and attributes them.

### Gaps
- minagric.gr blocks scripted access (403), so most Greek specifications were taken from Wines of Greece summaries and the EU dataset rather than the specifications. 541 Greek eAmbrosia attachments were downloaded but not mined systematically.
- Cyprus regional PDOs carry yields and varieties only.

## Switzerland: federal framework and cantonal AOCs (Vaud, Valais, Geneva)

### Takeaway
Federal law (OVin) sets AOC floors (whites 15.2 or 15.8 Brix, reds 17.0 Brix; 1.4 and 1.2 kg/m2), and the cantons regulate the rest. Valais revised its ordinance from 1 April 2026: classes are renamed AOC, vin de pays and vin de table, and Grand Cru minimums are higher (e.g. Chasselas 77.6 Oe, Arvine group 94.6 Oe).

### Cited Findings
- Federal classes, sugar floors, yield ceilings, the Reserve rule (18 months red, 12 white), blending tolerances and special terms ([OVin 916.140, Fedlex PDF](https://res.cloudinary.com/adminch/image/private/s--r4rN3e2K--/v1752031140/Bundespublikationen/916-140f.pdf)); 63 AOCs on the federal register ([BLW: vin](https://www.blw.admin.ch/fr/vin)).
- Valais AOC and Grand Cru sugar minimums, yields, traditional names (Fendant, Johannisberg, Dole, Heida) and Grand Cru rules (communal only, vines at least 8 years old) ([Valais OVV 916.142](https://lex.vs.ch/app/fr/texts_of_law/916.142)).
- Vaud regional AOCs and Grand cru (Dezaley, Calamin), Lavaux and the regulation of appellations ([Vaud RVV](https://www.lexfind.ch/tolv/261089/fr)); Geneva rules ([Geneva M 2 50.05](https://silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_m2_50p05.htm)).

### Inferences
- Swiss "Grand Cru" means different things in Vaud (an AOC name) and Valais (a communal quality mark on top of AOC Valais).

### Gaps
- Neuchatel, Ticino and the German-speaking cantons were not carded.

## England and Wales (Sussex PDO)

### Takeaway
The UK keeps retained EU GI law: English Wine and Welsh Wine PDOs and PGIs. Sussex (2022) is the first regional sparkling PDO, with traditional method and at least 15 months in bottle before release.

### Cited Findings
- Sussex: varieties, yields (12 t/ha, 80 hl/ha for sparkling), at least 12 months on lees plus 3 after disgorgement, 90% for single-variety sparkling, panel test ([Sussex product specification](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/64198e2ce90e0769f47f4755/Product_specification_for_Sussex_final_.docx)).
- English Wine PDO rules ([English Wine PDO specification](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5fd361f48fa8f54d6545da82/pfn-english-wine-pdo.pdf)); Darnibole ([GOV.UK: Darnibole](https://www.gov.uk/protected-food-drink-names/darnibole)).

### Inferences
- "British wine" (from imported must) falls outside these schemes.

### Gaps
- No current statement was found of any proposed reforms to the English Wine PDO.

## Georgia and Moldova

### Takeaway
Georgia's 20 or so appellations are micro-zones defined by variety, harvest sugar and yield (usually max 10 t/ha), registered by Sakpatenti and controlled by the National Wine Agency. Khvanchkara is naturally semi-sweet (30 to 45 g/l) from Aleksandrouli and Mujuretuli. Moldova uses four PGIs (Codru, Stefan Voda, Valul lui Traian, and the brandy PGI Divin).

### Cited Findings
- Specifications for 20 PDOs, e.g. Khvanchkara at least 24% grape sugar, 7 t/ha, 650 L/t ([National Wine Agency: Khvanchkara](https://wine.gov.ge/En/Wine/19)); index ([wine.gov.ge](https://wine.gov.ge/En/Wine)).
- Moldovan PGI specifications for Codru and Stefan Voda ([Codru order 215/2011](https://wineofmoldova.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/215_28-12-2011-min.pdf); [Stefan Voda order 188/2011](https://wineofmoldova.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/188_12-10-2011-min.pdf)).

### Inferences
- Qvevri is a method, not a legal category within the Georgian PDO specifications.

### Gaps
- The Valul lui Traian specification was not fetched. Georgian connections were intermittent, so some numbers come from a single fetch.

## Slovenia and Croatia

### Takeaway
Slovenia combines district PDOs with traditional PTP wines that have their own rules: Teran is 100% Refosk from the Karst plateau (9,000 kg/ha), and Cvicek is a light red-white blend at 8.5 to 10%. Croatia has kvalitetno and vrhunsko vino KZP with predicate wines from 94 Oe (Kasna berba) to 154 Oe (Izborna berba prosusenih bobica). Dingac is Plavac mali only, 48 hl/ha, with Klasiko and Riserva ageing tiers.

### Cited Findings
- Teran PTP: Refosk, 10.0 to 13.0% actual, at least 900 mg/l polyphenols, enrichment max 2% only with Karst Refosk concentrate or sucrose, 9,000 kg/ha ([eAmbrosia technical file: Teran](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/15984)).
- Cvicek PTP blend shares (Zametovka 40 to 50%, Kraljevina, Modra frankinja, Laski rizling), 8.5 to 10% ([eAmbrosia: Cvicek](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/15976)). Stajerska Slovenija: kakovostno and vrhunsko vino ZGP, pozna trgatev to suhi jagodni izbor, 8,000 l/ha ([eAmbrosia: Stajerska Slovenija](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/10327)).
- Croatian categories, predicate must weights, natural-alcohol minimums (9% and 10.5%) and yields ([eAmbrosia: Slavonija English specification](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/69104)). Hrvatska Istra: "Teran" name reserved, 12,000 and 11,000 kg/ha ([eAmbrosia: Hrvatska Istra](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/40081)).
- Dingac: Plavac mali crni, 48 hl/ha, goblet-trained vines, winemaking within Orebic and Trpanj, Klasiko 12 months (9 in wood) and Riserva 24 months (18 in wood), dark bottles only ([eAmbrosia: Dingac](https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/29915)).

### Inferences
- Both countries' specifications on eAmbrosia date from 2011 to 2017; later national amendments may have changed details.

### Gaps
- Slovenian predicate must weights (Oechsle) were not in the technical files read.
- No files were available for the Croatian Muskat momjanski or Ponikve PDOs.

## United States: AVA 85%, varietal 75%, vintage 85/95%, estate, Napa conjunctive labelling, Oregon, Washington, California, Meritage

### Takeaway
Federal rules (27 CFR Part 4) need 75% for a variety, state or county; 85% for an AVA (finished in the state); and 95% (AVA) or 85% for a vintage. States go further: California requires 100% California grapes, Oregon 90% varietal (75% for 18 listed varieties) and 95% from the appellation, and Washington 95% Washington. California's conjunctive laws make Napa, Sonoma County, Paso Robles and Lodi sub-AVAs name their parent. AB 1585 ("American" = 100% US grapes) stalled in the California Senate in June 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Varietal 75% (labrusca 51%), multiple varieties at 100% with percentages, appellation 75%, AVA 85% and finished in state, vintage 95% or 85%, Estate Bottled conditions, vineyard name 95%, alcohol tolerances ([27 CFR Part 4, eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-4)); about 280 AVAs ([27 CFR Part 9](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-9)).
- Semi-generic names usable only by brands with label approval before 10 March 2006 ([26 U.S.C. 5388](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/5388)).
- California 100% rule ([17 CCR 17015](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/17-CCR-17015)); Napa conjunctive labelling for wines bottled from 1 January 1990 ([B&P 25240](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_25240)); Napa name protection ([B&P 25241](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_25241)); Sonoma County from 2014 ([B&P 25246](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_25246)); Paso Robles from 2008 ([B&P 25244](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_25244)); Lodi from 2009 ([B&P 25245](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_25245)).
- Oregon 90% varietal with 18 exceptions at 75% ([OAR 845-010-0915](https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_845-010-0915)); 100% Oregon and 95% appellation ([OAR 845-010-0920](https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_845-010-0920)); "Willamette Valley" required with its nested AVAs from 2023 ([OAR 845-010-0923](https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_845-010-0923)).
- Washington: 95% Washington grapes when "Washington" is claimed (grapes harvested after 2009) ([RCW 66.28.110](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/RCW/RCW%20%2066%20%20TITLE/RCW%20%2066%20.%2028%20%20CHAPTER/RCW%20%2066%20.%2028%20.110.htm)).
- AB 1585: Senate committee hearing cancelled at the author's request on 23 June 2026 ([CalMatters Digital Democracy](https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260ab1585)); described as pulled after lobbying ([Wine Industry Advisor, 24 June 2026](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2026/06/24/american-wine-legislation-pulled-from-agenda-in-california-state-senate/)).
- Meritage: a licensed trademark; red from at least two of eight Bordeaux varieties and white from at least two of three, none over 90% ([Wikipedia: Meritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meritage)).

### Inferences
- In California, a sub-AVA wine effectively needs 100% California grapes and 85% from the sub-AVA, plus the conjunctive parent name for Napa, Sonoma County, Paso Robles and Lodi AVAs.

### Gaps
- The Meritage Alliance website blocks crawlers, so its terms come from Wikipedia.
- No statute or rule defines "Reserve" or "Old Vine" at federal level. The CFR has none, and no state definition was found.

## Canada: VQA Ontario, BC VQA, Quebec IGPs, Tidal Bay, icewine

### Takeaway
Quality schemes are provincial. In Ontario, sub-appellations need 100% and primary areas 85%, and Icewine requires picking at minus 8 C, 35 Brix average and at least 100 g/l. BC cut its regional-GI origin rule from 95% to 85% on 14 July 2025. Quebec's Vin de glace IGP needs at least 125 g/l.

### Cited Findings
- Ontario: origin tiers, varietal 85/90/95%, vintage 85%, Brix tables, Icewine and Late Harvest rules, sparkling rules (9 or 12 months on lees), Meritage, Icewine Dosage and Fracette ([O. Reg. 406/00, consolidated July 2026](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/000406)).
- BC: GI origin 85% from 14 July 2025 (previously 95%) ([B.C. Reg. 168/2018, point-in-time history](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2018_pit)); varietal 85% (two varieties 90% with 13.5% of the second), Icewine, Late Harvest Brix, Estate Bottled (10-year lease, from year 4), sweetness descriptors ([B.C. Reg. 168/2018](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2018)).
- Federal definition of icewine ([CFIA Standards of Identity Vol. 8](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/about-cfia/acts-and-regulations/list-acts-and-regulations/documents-incorporated-reference/canadian-standards-identity-volume-8)).
- Vin de glace du Quebec: reserved 30 December 2014; minus 8 C at harvest and pressing; 32 Brix per pressing and 35 in tank; at least 125 g/l; netting on the vine allowed ([CARTV specification v1.5](https://cartv.gouv.qc.ca/app/uploads/2022/12/cc_igpvinglaceqc_v1_5_06122022.pdf)). Cidre de glace: at least 140 g/l, 9 to 13% ([CARTV](https://cartv.gouv.qc.ca/app/uploads/2022/02/cc_cdg_qc_igp_v20220217_complet.pdf)). Vin du Quebec IGP (2018), 8 to 15% ([CARTV fact sheet](https://cartv.gouv.qc.ca/app/uploads/2021/06/fiche_vin-du-quebec_2021-1.pdf)).
- Tidal Bay: 100% Nova Scotia grapes, at most 11%, approved yearly by a blind panel, launched June 2012 ([Wine Growers Nova Scotia](https://winesofnovascotia.ca/discover-nova-scotia-wine/tidal-bay/)).

### Inferences
- After July 2025, a BC regional-GI wine can contain 15% from elsewhere in BC, which brings it in line with Ontario's primary areas.

### Gaps
- The full Tidal Bay standard (variety list, sugar range) is not published on the association site.

## Australia: 85% rules, LIP, GIs, Rutherglen Muscat, Apera/Topaque

### Takeaway
One variety, vintage or GI needs 85%; two or three GIs need 95% with at least 5% each. The Label Integrity Program requires seven years of records. GIs are boundaries only. Rutherglen's Muscat and Topaque tiers (Rutherglen, Classic, Grand, Rare) are a voluntary producer code.

### Cited Findings
- Sections 25 to 27 blending rules and the GI criteria (500 t, five independent 5 ha vineyards) ([Wine Australia Regulations 2018, compilation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2018L00286/latest/text)); summary ([Wine Australia: blending rules](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/further-information/the-blending-rules)).
- LIP record-keeping ([Wine Australia: LIP](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/label-integrity-program)).
- Apera (from 2011) and Topaque (from 2020) certification trade marks; Cabernets, Pinots and Moscato at 85%; icewine definition ([Wine Australia: additional labelling terms](https://www.wineaustralia.com/labelling/additional-labelling-terms)); fortified terms (Vintage at least 20 months before release) ([Quality Wine Terms PDF](https://www.wineaustralia.com/getmedia/6fa35690-95eb-4519-a6f8-aae0359aa822/AGWA-Quality-Wine-Terms-Quality-Wine-Terms.pdf)).
- Minimum 4.5% for wine and 15 to 22% for fortified wine; water rules (dilution not below 13.5 Baume) ([Food Standards Code 4.5.1](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2008B00809/latest/text)).
- Rutherglen classification average ages: 3 to 5, 6 to 10, 11 to 19 and over 20 years; Muscat a Petits Grains Rouge only ([Winemakers of Rutherglen](https://winemakers.com.au/muscat-of-rutherglen/)); Topaque from Muscadelle with the same tiers ([Winemakers of Rutherglen: Topaque](https://winemakers.com.au/topaque-of-rutherglen/)).
- Barossa Old Vine Charter (2009): Old Vine 35, Survivor 70, Centenarian 100, Ancestor 125 years ([Vinehealth Australia](https://vinehealth.com.au/2018/11/celebrating-our-old-vines/)).

### Inferences
- "Single vineyard" and "Old Vine" (outside the Barossa charter) have no legal definition in Australia.

### Gaps
- GI-level cards were limited to Rutherglen, Barossa Valley, Australia and South Eastern Australia; all other GIs inherit the national card.

## New Zealand

### Takeaway
Variety, vintage and origin claims need 85% (reg 43, Wine Regulations 2021). A registered GI also needs any remainder to be New Zealand grapes (GI Act s. 21(ab)).

### Cited Findings
- Reg 43 (85%, descending order, export exemption), reg 44 (icewine) ([Wine Regulations 2021](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2021/401/en/latest/)).
- GI Act s. 21 85% rule and New Zealand remainder ([GI Act 2006, version 1 May 2024](https://legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2006/60/en/latest/)); the 2015 MPI proposal behind it ([MPI discussion paper 2015/27](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/dmsdocument/8686-Wine-Geographical-Indications-Proposed-amendment-to-the-85-rule-in-section-21)).

### Inferences
- New Zealand GIs set no production rules, so only the national card is provided.

### Gaps
- The date when s. 21(ab) came into force was not confirmed.

## South Africa: Wine of Origin, Cap Classique, Heritage seal, CWG

### Takeaway
A WO origin claim requires 100% of grapes from the unit; cultivar and vintage claims need 85%. MCC became a separate class from the 2023 harvest (whole-bunch pressing, 12 months on lees, certified), with a 36-month Extended Lees Aging class.

### Cited Findings
- WO scheme: 100% origin with narrow exceptions, cultivar 85% (since 2006), vintage 85%, estate and single vineyard (max 6 ha) ([Wine of Origin Scheme, SAWIS](https://www.sawis.co.za/winelaw/download/Origin_Scheme,_annotated_08_2022.pdf)).
- Wine classes and sugar levels: Noble Late Harvest at least 28 Balling and over 50 g/l; MCC 12 months; MCC Extended Lees Aging 36 months from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier; Cape port and sherry-style classes ([Liquor Products Act Regulations, Table 2](https://www.acts.co.za/liquor-products-act-1989/regs_r1433_table_2__classes_for_wine_and_specific_requirements_for_classes.php)).
- 2025 amendments: single-vineyard field blends, a "single vine" category, class designation on the main label from 31 December 2027 ([SAWIS Wine Law 1 of 2025](https://www.sawis.co.za/winelaw/download/Amendments_to_Regulations_and_W_O_Scheme_final.pdf)).
- Certified Heritage Vineyards seal for vines of 35 years or more, with the planting year on the seal ([TopWineSA, April 2018](https://topwinesa.com/2018/04/a-world-first-south-africa-introduces-old-vine-hertitage-certification/); [Old Vine Project](https://www.oldvineproject.co.za/)).
- Cape Winemakers Guild: founded 1982, first auction 1985, 42 members ([Decanter, 3 October 2025](https://www.decanter.com/wine-news/cape-winemakers-guild-auction-showcases-a-new-era-of-top-south-african-wines-566524)).

### Inferences
- Plain "traditional method" South African sparkling may still be made on 9 months' lees but may not be called MCC.

### Gaps
- Ward-level cards were not built; all WO units inherit the national card.

## Chile: DO rules, Costa/Entre Cordilleras/Andes, quality terms

### Takeaway
Chile's DO decree (464/1994, amended) uses 75% for origin, variety and vintage, and 85% for the Costa, Entre Cordilleras and Andes complements. Reserva needs 0.5% above the 11.5% legal minimum and Gran Reserva 1% above plus oak. A "vino bajo alcohol" category (at least 8.5%) was created in September 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 75% rules, Costa/Entre Cordilleras/Andes area lists, Embotellado en Origen, sweetness terms and quality terms ([Decreto 464, SAG consolidated text](https://sag.cl/sites/default/files/decreto_ndeg_464.pdf)).
- 11.5% legal minimum for wine and the new "vino bajo alcohol" ([Decreto 68/2026, Diario Oficial 11 September 2026](https://www.diariooficial.interior.gob.cl/publicaciones/2026/09/11/44548/01/2867706.pdf)).
- Secano Interior extended to seven Biobio communes ([Decreto 4/2026, Diario Oficial 14 July 2026](https://www.diariooficial.interior.gob.cl/publicaciones/2026/07/14/44499/01/2835807.pdf)); Chiloe and Rapa Nui from 1 March 2025 ([Decreto 27/2024](https://www.diariooficial.interior.gob.cl/publicaciones/2025/05/14/44148/01/2643653.pdf)).
- Los Lingues DO upheld by the Supreme Court in June 2026 ([Actualidad Juridica](https://actualidadjuridica.doe.cl/corte-suprema-confirma-rechazo-de-nulidad-contra-la-denominacion-de-origen-los-lingues/)).

### Inferences
- With the minimum at 11.5%, Reserva means at least 12.0% and Gran Reserva at least 12.5%. These follow from the decree's "above the legal minimum" wording.

### Gaps
- The decrees creating Apalta, Licanten and Lo Abarca were not seen. The SAG consolidated PDF (2016) predates them.

## Argentina: IP/IG/DOC, Lujan de Cuyo, Reserva

### Takeaway
Ley 25.163 sets IP (80%), IG (grown, made and bottled in the area) and DOC tiers; variety and vintage need 85%. Reserva requires 12 months (reds), and Gran Reserva 18 months for reds since 2019. DOC Lujan de Cuyo (1989) requires at least 85% Malbec and 18 months including 6 in oak.

### Cited Findings
- IP, IG and DOC definitions; cross-IG blends may not use an IG ([Ley 25.163](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/arg65345.pdf)); 110 IGs and 2 DOCs in 2023, and only Lujan de Cuyo used in 2022 ([INV report, July 2023](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2018/12/vinos_con_denominacion_de_origen_0.pdf)).
- Variety 85%, vintage 85% ([INV labelling manual 2019](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/manual_de_etiquetado-diciembre_2019_2.pdf)); Reserva and Gran Reserva ([Res. C.11/2011](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-180304/texto)); Gran Reserva reds 18 months ([Res. 14/2019 art. 7](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/arg192132.pdf)); variety list ([Res. 30/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-402989/texto)).
- Lujan de Cuyo DOC rules (85% Malbec, 18 months incl. 6 in oak, 800 to 1,100 m, 5,000 vines/ha) ([The Drinks Business, 6 October 2023](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2023/10/how-the-oldest-doc-in-america-is-getting-a-facelift/)).

### Inferences
- The INV manual still shows 18 months for Gran Reserva reds, consistent with Res. 14/2019, which superseded the 24 months in C.11/2011.

### Gaps
- DOC San Rafael production rules were not found. The Lujan de Cuyo reglamento itself was not fetched (trade-press source only).

## Brazil and Uruguay

### Takeaway
Brazil has two wine DOs. Vale dos Vinhedos (2012) requires Merlot-led reds (at least 60%), Chardonnay-led whites, 10 t/ha and 12 months before release for reds. Altos de Pinto Bandeira (2022) is traditional-method sparkling only, with 12 months on lees. Uruguay has no DO system: VCP wines need 12% and 85% variety and vintage claims.

### Cited Findings
- Vale dos Vinhedos regulamento (OCR of the scanned INPI file): varieties, 10 t/ha and 12 t/ha, grape potential alcohol 12% and 11%, no chaptalisation, reds 12 months and whites 6 months from 1 March, sparkling 9 months on lees, bottling in the area ([INPI: Vale dos Vinhedos DO](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/indicacoes-geograficas/arquivos/cadernos-de-especificacoes-tecnicas/ValedosVinhedosDO.pdf)); context ([Embrapa 2015](https://www.cnpuv.embrapa.br/cadastro-viticola/do-vale-dos-vinhedos/site/pdf/capituloI.pdf)).
- Altos de Pinto Bandeira: Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Riesling Italico (max 25%), 12 t/ha, at least 14 degrees Babo, 65% press yield, 12 months from tirage to disgorgement, Reserva 24 and Gran Reserva 36 months ([INPI specification](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/indicacoes-geograficas/arquivos/cadernos-de-especificacoes-tecnicas/AltosdePintoBandeira.pdf)); granted 29 November 2022 ([INPI certificate](https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/indicacoes-geograficas/arquivos/certificados-de-ig/certificado_br4120210000034_altos-de-pinto-bandeira.pdf)).
- Uruguay VCP: glass bottles up to 750 ml, 85% variety and vintage, two varieties only at 100% ([Decreto 283/993](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/decretos/283-1993)); VCP minimum 12% from 2007 ([Decreto 9/2007](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/decretos/9-2007)).

### Inferences
- The Vale dos Vinhedos regulamento is a scanned document; one number (white release, 6 months) was read from the page image because OCR missed it.

### Gaps
- Brazilian IP specifications (Campanha Gaucha, Vale do Sao Francisco and others) were not carded. Uruguay's informal regional zones have no legal rules to card.

## Japan and China

### Takeaway
Japan's 2018 labelling standard reserves place, variety and vintage claims for "Japan Wine" (100% Japanese grapes) at 85%. Its five prefectural wine GIs add variety lists, grape-sugar minimums, chaptalisation caps and in-prefecture bottling. China's standard GB/T 15037 uses 80% for vintage and origin and 75% for variety. Ningxia's Helan Mountain East runs China's only official winery classification (five tiers since 2016, re-scored every two years).

### Cited Findings
- Japan Wine definition and 85% rules ([NTA wine label pamphlet](https://WWW.NTA.GO.JP/publication/pamph/sake/winelabel_2.pdf); [WTO TBT draft text, 2015](https://members.wto.org/crnattachments/2015/TBT/JPN/15_3013_00_e.pdf)).
- GI specifications: Yamanashi (Koshu at least 14% sugar, vinifera 18%; added sugar caps; bottled in Yamanashi) ([NTA: Yamanashi](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/02_01.htm)); Hokkaido (max 14.5% abv) ([NTA: Hokkaido](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/02_07.htm)); Yamagata ([NTA](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/02_3.htm)); Osaka ([NTA](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/02_15.htm)); Nagano with a Premium tier ([NTA](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/02_16.htm)); GI dates ([NTA GI list](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/geographical/02.htm)).
- China: vintage 80%, variety 75%, origin 80% and sweetness bands in the GB/T 15037 revision draft, whose list of changes leaves these definitions untouched ([GB/T 15037 draft](https://file.smejs.cn/file/group1/M00/00/19/rBIAAWaHU2-AHWtZAAYpPQfvdvQ852.pdf)); GB/T 15037-2006 in force ([ChemLinked](https://food.chemlinked.com/database/view/2814)).
- Ningxia classification: begun 2013, five tiers from 2016, biennial review, own-vineyard and estate-bottling conditions ([Decanter China, 2017](https://www.decanterchina.com/en/news/ningxia-releases-latest-winery-classification-listing)); 57 classified wineries in 2021 with no First Growth ([China Daily, 27 September 2021](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202109/27/WS6151bd58a310cdd39bc6be42.html)); four Chinese wine GIs protected in the EU ([Grape Wall of China](https://www.grapewallofchina.com/2021/03/01/gi-jiu-eu-china-pact-on-geographical-indications-protects-wine/)).

### Inferences
- The 2006 thresholds are inferred to equal the draft's, because the draft's change list does not include those definitions.

### Gaps
- The final Japanese NTA notice text was not fetched; the WTO draft and the NTA pamphlet agree on the 85% rules.
- No Ningxia classification list after 2021 was found.
